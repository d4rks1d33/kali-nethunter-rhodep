KEY LIVE FINDINGS (S3FWRN5, rhodep, listen/card-emulation):
- The RF listen front-end DOES light up, but ONLY when the NFC-F/NFC-DEP peer is armed.
  * With NFC-DEP peer active (LF_PROTOCOL_TYPE=default, LMRT routes NFC-F/DEP to host):
    chip activates repeatedly as NFC-F LISTEN (activation_rf_tech_and_mode 0x82,
    rf_protocol 0x5 NFC-DEP, rf_interface 0x1 FRAME).
  * With NFC-DEP suppressed (LF_PROTOCOL_TYPE=0 + LMRT without NFC-F/DEP entries):
    chip activates NOTHING (0 activations). BOTH host and eSE paths.
- So: the DEP peer is what powers the analog listen front-end. We must KEEP it to
  light the RF, but steer the NFC-A/MIFARE field to the eSE (NFCEE 0x83, proto MIFARE).
- eSE = NFCEE 0x83, sole protocol 0x80 (MIFARE). NFCEE_MODE_SET(0x83,ENABLE) accepted
  once (0x0), later 0x3 REJECTED because already enabled (now made conditional).
- RF_DISCOVER offers NFC-A listen 0x80 + NFC-F listen 0x82.
- Reader (real NFC reader) response so far: unknown/nothing conclusive.
The QUESTION: how does the Android HAL make NFC-A/MIFARE reach the eSE (so a reader
sees the SUBE MIFARE card) WHILE the NFC-F/DEP peer stays armed to power the RF?
